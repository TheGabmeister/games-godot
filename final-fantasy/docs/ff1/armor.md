# Final Fantasy I Pixel Remaster — Armor

Complete armor list (40 pieces across 4 categories). Classes use abbreviations: Wa=Warrior, Kn=Knight, Th=Thief, Ni=Ninja, Mo=Monk, Ma=Master, RM=Red Mage, RW=Red Wizard, WM=White Mage, WW=White Wizard, BM=Black Mage, BW=Black Wizard.

Each piece adds to Absorb (physical defense) and penalizes Evade% (evasion). Heavier armor = more defense but lower evasion.
